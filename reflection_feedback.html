Reflection feedback on V1
The original chart effectively compares Q1 coffee sales between 2024 and 2025 using a clear grouped bar chart. The title, axis labels, and legend are appropriate and easy to understand. The use of gridlines on the y-axis aids in reading values, and the x-axis label rotation prevents overlap. For improvement, adding direct value labels on top of each bar would allow for more precise reading of the sales figures without relying solely on the gridlines. Additionally, enhancing the font sizes for better readability and adding a currency symbol to the y-axis label would improve the overall presentation.
